CPCV

Combinatorial Purged Cross-Validation: re-backtests one strategy across every combinatorial train/test partition of purged, embargoed groups, instead of trusting a single train/test split. A strategy whose edge only survives ONE particular split is a red flag this catches that a plain holdout can miss.
